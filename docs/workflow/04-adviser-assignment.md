# Adviser Assignment Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow describes how a capstone group formally assigns an adviser within Archive.

The selection of the adviser is **not performed by the system**. The decision is made outside Archive, either by the Coordinator or by the capstone group, depending on the academic process followed by the institution.

Once an adviser has been chosen, the group sends an invitation through Archive. Upon accepting the invitation, the faculty member becomes the group's official adviser and gains access to adviser-specific features.

---

# Actors

* Student (Group Leader)
* Faculty Member (Adviser)

---

# Preconditions

Before this workflow begins:

* The capstone group has been successfully formed.
* The adviser has already been selected outside the system.
* The selected faculty member has an Archive account.
* The selected faculty member has joined the faculty within Archive.
* The group has not yet assigned an adviser.

---

# Workflow

## Part A – Adviser Invitation

1. The Group Leader navigates to the **Adviser Management** page.
2. The Group Leader selects the previously chosen faculty member from the list of available faculty members.
3. The Group Leader sends an adviser invitation.
4. The system notifies the selected faculty member.

---

## Part B – Adviser Response

1. The faculty member reviews the adviser invitation.
2. The faculty member accepts the invitation.
3. The system assigns the faculty member as the group's adviser.
4. Adviser-specific features become available.
5. The adviser gains access to the group's milestone workspace, submissions, and progress.
6. The group may begin adviser consultations and chapter submissions.

---

# Postconditions

After successful completion:

* The capstone group has an assigned adviser.
* The adviser gains access to adviser-exclusive features.
* The adviser may review submissions, provide feedback, and monitor the group's progress.
* The group becomes eligible to begin the adviser consultation workflow.

---

# Alternate Flows

## AF-01: Adviser Already Assigned

If the capstone group already has an assigned adviser, the system prevents additional adviser invitations.

---

## AF-02: Invitation Declined

If the faculty member declines the adviser invitation, no adviser is assigned.

The group must send a new invitation after another adviser has been selected outside the system.

---

## AF-03: Invitation Already Pending

If an invitation has already been sent to the selected faculty member and is still pending, the system prevents duplicate invitations.

---

# Business Notes

* Archive does not determine or recommend advisers.
* Adviser selection occurs outside the system based on the institution's academic process.
* The adviser may be chosen by the Coordinator or by the capstone group.
* A capstone group may have only one active adviser.
* An adviser may supervise multiple capstone groups.
* Adviser-exclusive features become available only after the invitation has been accepted.

---

# Result

The selected faculty member accepts the adviser invitation and becomes the official adviser of the capstone group, enabling the consultation, document review, and feedback processes within Archive.
