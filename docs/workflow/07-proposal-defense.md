# Proposal Defense Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow describes the Proposal Defense process for Capstone 1. It covers defense scheduling, panel assignment, document submission, panel evaluation, defense verdicts, and the revision process until the proposal is approved.

---

# Actors

* Coordinator
* Student (Capstone Group)
* Panelist

---

# Preconditions

Before this workflow begins:

* The capstone group has completed Chapters 1, 2, and 3.
* All required chapters have been approved by the assigned adviser.
* The capstone group is eligible for the Proposal Defense.

---

# Workflow

## Part A – Defense Scheduling

1. The Coordinator creates the Proposal Defense schedule.

2. The Coordinator assigns the following panelists to the capstone group:

   * Chairperson
   * Panel Member 1
   * Panel Member 2

3. The system notifies the assigned panelists and the capstone group.

4. The capstone group may view the defense schedule and assigned panelists.

---

## Part B – Proposal Submission

1. The capstone group uploads the compiled Proposal Manuscript (Chapters 1–3).

2. The system validates and records the submission.

3. The assigned panelists gain access to the submitted manuscript.

---

## Part C – Defense Evaluation

1. During the Proposal Defense, the assigned panelists review the submitted manuscript.

2. Panelists may annotate the document and provide comments.

3. After the evaluation, the panel determines one of the following verdicts:

* Approved
* Approved with Minor Revisions
* Approved with Major Revisions
* Redefense

4. The system records the verdict and notifies the capstone group.

---

## Part D – Revision Process

If the verdict is **Approved with Minor Revisions** or **Approved with Major Revisions**:

1. The capstone group addresses all required revisions.

2. The group uploads the revised manuscript.

3. The system notifies all assigned panelists.

4. Each panelist reviews the revised submission.

5. Once all assigned panelists approve the revised manuscript, the Proposal Defense is marked as completed.

---

# Postconditions

After successful completion:

* The Proposal Defense is completed.
* All required revisions have been addressed and approved.
* The Capstone 1 milestone is marked as completed.
* The capstone group becomes eligible for Capstone 2.

---

# Alternate Flows

## AF-01: Proposal Redefense

If the panel issues a **Redefense** verdict, the Proposal Defense is marked as unsuccessful and the coordinator may schedule a new defense for the group.

The succeeding actions shall follow the academic policies established by the institution.

---

## AF-02: Revision Required

If the panel requires revisions, the Proposal Defense remains incomplete until all assigned panelists approve the revised manuscript.

---

# Business Notes

* Only the Coordinator may schedule Proposal Defenses.
* Every Proposal Defense must have one Chairperson and two Panel Members.
* Students must upload the compiled Proposal Manuscript before the scheduled defense.
* Panelists may annotate and comment on the submitted manuscript.
* All assigned panelists must approve revised submissions before the Proposal Defense is completed.
* Capstone 2 cannot begin until the Proposal Defense has been successfully completed.
